STOCK MANAGER: HOW TO PUT IT ON YOUR LAPTOPS AND PHONES
=========================================================

What you have
-------------
This folder is the whole app. Once it is on a web address, every laptop and phone
can open that address and "install" it like a normal app. After the first visit it
works with NO internet.

IMPORTANT: each device keeps its OWN data. A sale entered on one phone does not
appear on another phone or laptop. Use one device per till, or ask for the shared
version if everyone must see the same stock.


PART 1. PUT THE FOLDER ON A WEB ADDRESS (do this once, on a computer)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You need a free host. Any of these work:
  - Netlify (netlify.com): create a free account, then choose to deploy a site by
    dragging and dropping a folder. Drop this whole "stock-manager-app" folder.
  - Cloudflare Pages (pages.cloudflare.com): choose "Upload assets" and upload the folder.
  - GitHub Pages: upload the files to a new repository and turn on Pages.
(Menu names change from time to time. Look for "deploy manually", "drag and drop" or
"upload".)

Afterwards the host gives you an address such as https://yourname.netlify.app
You can usually rename it in the site settings. The address must start with https.

Keep the address to your staff. The app asks for passwords, but anyone who knows the
address can open their own empty copy of it.


PART 2. MOVE YOUR EXISTING DATA (only if you already use the file version)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the OLD version, log in as owner, go to Data, click "Download full backup".
2. Open the NEW address on the same computer, go to Data, use "Restore backup".
3. Set your owner password again under Data. Passwords are not copied from a backup.
Do this on each device that needs the old data.


PART 3. INSTALL ON EACH DEVICE
------------------------------
ANDROID PHONE (Chrome)
  1. Open your address in Chrome.
  2. Tap the "Install app on this device" button at the bottom of the screen, or open
     the Chrome menu (three dots) and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Tap Install. The Stock Manager icon appears on your home screen.

IPHONE OR IPAD (must be Safari)
  1. Open your address in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
  3. Always open the app from that icon. Installing it this way also stops iOS from
     clearing your saved data when you do not use the app for a while.

WINDOWS OR MAC LAPTOP (Chrome or Edge)
  1. Open your address.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or the
     "Install app on this device" button in the app. In Chrome you can also use the
     menu: Cast, save and share > Install page as app. In Edge: Apps > Install this site as an app.
  3. Click Install. It opens in its own window and gets a desktop shortcut.

MAC (Safari): File > Add to Dock.


PART 4. EVERY DAY
-----------------
- Open the app from its icon. It works with or without internet.
- Each device: set the owner password (Data menu), then the accountant, warehouse
  manager and sales personnel passwords, then click Log out.
- Download a backup every week (Data > Download full backup) and keep it on a USB
  drive or in cloud storage. If a device is lost, reset or its browser data is cleared,
  the backup is the only way to get your records back.


PART 5. UPDATING THE APP
------------------------
When you get a new version of this folder, upload it to the same host again, replacing
the old files. Each device picks up the new version the next time it is opened with
internet. If it still shows the old one, close the app completely and open it again.
Your data is not touched by an update.
